CURRICULUM & CLASSIFICATION

Crystallography Subject & Material Classes

Comprehensive matrix of 10 benchmark material classes, crystal symmetry systems, Wyckoff coordinates, and MESP relaxation principles.

💎

1. Corundum & Trigonal Oxides

Compounds such as Fe₂O₃ (Hematite) and Al₂O₃ crystallize in space group R-3c (#167). Cations occupy octahedral Wyckoff 12c sites, while oxygen anions occupy 18e positions. High hardness, magnetic exchange splitting, and stable ground states.

Space Group #167
⚡

2. Doped Solid Solutions & Substitutions

Substitutional solid solutions like Fe₁.₉Ti₀.₁O₃ or co-doped Fe₁.₈₅Ti₀.₁Cr₀.₀₅O₃ preserve the host parent lattice while modifying local ligand field splitting and dielectric response. Evaluated via supercell expansion (2x2x2 or 3x3x1) and MACE-MP-0 float64.

Doped Systems
🔄

3. Polymorphic Systems

Compounds exhibiting multiple allotropic phases such as TiO₂ (Rutile P4₂/mnm vs Anatase I4₁/amd vs Brookite Pbca) and C (Graphite vs Diamond). The pipeline performs polymorphism screening, stability ranking, and relative ΔE comparison.

Polymorphism
🧂

4. Rock-Salt & Fluorite Halides

Includes NaCl (Rock-salt, Fm-3m, 6:6 coordination) and CaF₂ (Fluorite, Fm-3m, 8:4 coordination). Highly symmetric ionic lattices characterized by sharp Madelung potentials and minimal relaxation displacement.

Fm-3m Face-Centered
💻

5. Diamond & Elemental Semiconductors

Elemental systems such as Si (Fd-3m, diamond cubic) and Cu (Fm-3m, FCC). Serves as baseline calibration standards for atomic radii, lattice parameter accuracy, and pseudo-potential cutoff verification.

Group IV / Metals
🥞

6. 2D Dichalcogenides & Wurtzite

Layered transition metal dichalcogenides (MoS₂, P6₃/mmc) with van der Waals interlayer gaps, and non-centrosymmetric semiconductors (ZnO, P6₃mc wurtzite) with intrinsic spontaneous polarization.

Layered & Polar
