SECONDARY & GENERATIVE CHANNELS

Other-Source Repositories & CrystaLLM Fallback

Tier-2 and tier-3 structure resolution when on-source entries do not match: Crystallography Open Database (COD), AFLOW, OQMD, and CrystaLLM generative models.

🌐

Crystallography Open Database (COD)

Over 500,000 experimental crystal structures from peer-reviewed literature. Queried via SQL and HTTP REST when theoretical DFT models require experimental cross-validation.

Type: ExperimentalCOD #ID
⚡

AFLOW & OQMD Federations

High-throughput automated computational materials databases. Used as secondary fallback when stoichiometric variants or unusual pressure/temperature phases are queried.

Type: High-ThroughputFederated
🤖

CrystaLLM Autoregressive Generator

Transformer-based generative model generating valid CIF text directly for novel, unindexed, or hypothetical compounds with looping validation and MACE-MP-0 relaxation.

Type: Generative AICrystaLLM
